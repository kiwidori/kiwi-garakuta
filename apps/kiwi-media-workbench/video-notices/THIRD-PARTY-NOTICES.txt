FFmpeg is copyright the FFmpeg developers. This is an independent unofficial GUI.
No FFmpeg executables, libraries, build archive, or third-party codecs are distributed in this GUI ZIP.
The first-use button downloads an original archive directly from gyan.dev / GyanD/codexffmpeg, which is linked
by the FFmpeg download page: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
The exact download URL, size, checksums, and member checksums are recorded in upstream-pin.json.
The downloaded archive's files, LICENSE.txt, and documentation remain in the local engine directory.
FFmpeg-REFERENCE-LICENSE.txt is a reference copy of its GPLv3 license, not the license of this GUI.
The engine is invoked as a separate process. Users can replace its local executables and libraries;
runtime loading does not lock replacement binaries to the pinned hashes.
